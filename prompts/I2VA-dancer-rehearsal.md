### I2VA: Dancer Rehearsal

> Official video guide example — describe animation from first frame

#### 📖 Description

A dancer in a rehearsal room performs a contemporary dance sequence. This prompt demonstrates the I2VA technique: preserve everything visible in the first frame (dancer, room, lighting), then describe only what changes (movements, camera path). Don't repeat what the image already shows.

#### 📝 Prompt

```text
For the target video, at 00.00 seconds into the target video, <Picture 1> (from [Shot 1]) is fully referenced.

integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Live-action, cinematic, the female dancer in a black leotard shown in <Picture 1> remains in the same rehearsal room with the same wooden floor, mirror wall, and soft overhead lighting. The camera holds a static shot as she completes a contemporary dance turn, her arms extending outward. She then glides laterally across the floor in a smooth sequence, ending in a stable final pose with one arm raised. The camera tracks right with small amplitude at slow speed, following her movement across the space. Her breathing is visible but controlled.

overall_soundscape: Soft fabric movement, bare feet sliding on polished wood, steady breathing, and the faint hum of fluorescent lights.

non_diegetic_music: A minimal ambient texture at a slow tempo — sustained electronic pads with no rhythmic elements, creating a meditative atmosphere.
```
