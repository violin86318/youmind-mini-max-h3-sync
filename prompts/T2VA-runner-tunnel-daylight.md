### T2VA: Runner Tunnel to Daylight

> Official camera movement example — static hold then reveal

#### 📖 Description

A runner emerges from a dark tunnel into bright daylight. This prompt demonstrates the technique of using a static camera hold followed by a single deliberate pan to reveal new information — a core H3 cinematography principle.

#### 📝 Prompt

```text
integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Cinematic, sports documentary style, a wide static shot frames the dark mouth of a concrete tunnel. A female runner in a red tank top and black shorts sprints toward the camera from within the tunnel. The camera holds completely still as she exits the tunnel into blinding daylight, slowing to a jog. [Shot 2] At 00:06.000, the camera pans right with large amplitude at normal speed, revealing an open running track stretching toward a forested hillside. The runner continues forward along the track. The camera holds the static shot as she grows smaller in the frame.

overall_soundscape: Footsteps on concrete transitioning to gravel, heavy breathing, distant birdsong, and a faint breeze through trees.

non_diegetic_music: An energetic but minimalist percussion loop at a moderate tempo, with a bright synth arpeggio that enters as daylight hits.
```

#### 📌 Details

- **Type:** T2VA
- **Duration:** ~10 seconds
- **Source:** [Official camera movement guide](https://minimaxh3.tv/zh/blog/minimax-h3-prompt-guide) (adapted)
- **Key technique:** Static hold → pan reveal (camera movement for information reveal, not decoration)
- **Tags:** sports, cinematic, outdoor, camera-reveal, runner
