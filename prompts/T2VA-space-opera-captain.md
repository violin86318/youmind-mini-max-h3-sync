### T2VA: Space Opera Captain

> Official V2 API example — sci-fi with emotional dialogue

#### 📖 Description

A space opera scene where a fleet captain watches her last ships jump to warp. The scene combines event sequencing (the fleet departure), emotional acting (her reflection), and a defined ending state (empty stars). Based on the official V2 API example.

#### 📝 Prompt

```text
integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Sci-fi cinematic, a medium shot frames a female captain standing at the observation window of a starship bridge. Behind her through the glass, a fleet of ships glows against deep space. The camera holds a static shot as the first ship flares blue and vanishes into warp. Then another. Then the last. The bridge shudders slightly. Her reflection in the glass shows tired eyes. The captain with a low, steady voice (S1) says: <d>[English] Safe travels.</d> [Shot 2] At 00:08.000, the camera cuts to a close-up as the camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed toward her reflection in the window. The glass now shows only empty stars. She exhales slowly. The shot holds on the empty starfield.

overall_soundscape: Low ship-engine hum, the deep thrum of warp drives engaging, a subtle bridge alert tone, and the quiet hiss of life support.

non_diegetic_music: A slow, melancholic synthesizer pad at a low volume, with a single repeating piano note that fades into silence.
```
